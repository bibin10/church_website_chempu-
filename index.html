Apostolic Heritage

Seven Centuries of Divine Grace

The historic St. Thomas Catholic Church of Chempu represents a venerable legacy of St. Thomas Christians (Nasranis) nestled in Kottayam district.

Centuries of Faith in Central Travancore

Established in early fourteenth century (1307 AD), St. Thomas Church Chempu is one of the revered historical churches under the Syro-Malabar Major Archiepiscopal Archdiocese of Ernakulam-Angamaly.

Surrounded by coconut groves and picturesque backwaters of the Vembanad region near Vaikom, the parish has nurtured generations of devoted families, preserved authentic East-Syriac liturgical customs, and sustained community welfare for centuries.

The annual parish festival is marked with high spiritual solemnity. The traditional "Kodiyettu" (flag hoisting), Raza Qurbana, grand candle-lit processions carrying holy relics and traditional silk parasols (Muthukkuda), and fellowship meals unite the entire village of Chempu.
